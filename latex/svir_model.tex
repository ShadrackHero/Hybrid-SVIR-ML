\section{The SVIR model}

The full system of nonlinear ordinary differential equations is
\begin{subequations} \label{eq:SVIR}
\begin{align}
    \dfrac{dS}{dt} &= \pi + \sigma V - \lambda S - \omega S - \mu S, \label{eq:SVIR_S}\\
    \dfrac{dV}{dt} &= \omega S - (1 - \epsilon) \lambda V - \sigma V - \mu V, \label{eq:SVIR_V}\\
    \dfrac{dI}{dt} &= \lambda S + (1 - \epsilon) \lambda V - (\mu + \gamma + \rho_1 + \delta) I, \label{eq:SVIR_I}\\
    \dfrac{dI_c}{dt} &= \gamma I - (\mu + \rho_2 + \delta) I_c, \label{eq:SVIR_Ic}\\
    \dfrac{dR}{dt} &= \rho_1 I + \rho_2 I_c - \mu R. \label{eq:SVIR_R}
\end{align}
\end{subequations}

Each term on the right-hand side of \eqref{eq:SVIR} corresponds to a transition in the compartmental flow. The system is supplemented with the initial conditions
\begin{equation}
\begin{aligned}
    S(0)&=S_0>0, &
    V(0)&=V_0\geq 0, &
    I(0)&=I_0\geq 0, \\
    I_c(0)&=I_{c,0}\geq 0, &
    R(0)&=R_0\geq 0.
\end{aligned}
\label{eq:initial_conditions}
\end{equation}

All parameters are non-negative, with \(\pi,\mu,\beta>0\) and \(0\leq\epsilon\leq 1\). Their meaning and units are given in Table~\ref{tab:parameters}.

\begin{table}[ht]
\centering
\caption{Parameters of the SVIR model.}
\label{tab:parameters}
\rowcolors{2}{lightgray}{white}
\begin{tabular}{llp{4.2cm}}
\toprule
\textbf{Parameter} & \textbf{Description} & \textbf{Units} \\
\midrule
\(\pi\)        & Recruitment (birth) rate into \(S\) & individuals\(\cdot\)day\(^{-1}\) \\
\(\mu\)        & Natural death rate & day\(^{-1}\) \\
\(\omega\)     & Vaccination rate (\(S\to V\)) & day\(^{-1}\) \\
\(\sigma\)     & Waning rate of vaccine immunity (\(V\to S\)) & day\(^{-1}\) \\
\(\epsilon\)   & Vaccine efficacy against infection & dimensionless \\
\(\gamma\)     & Detection rate (\(I\to I_c\)) & day\(^{-1}\) \\
\(\rho_1\)     & Recovery rate of undetected infectious individuals & day\(^{-1}\) \\
\(\rho_2\)     & Recovery rate of confirmed infectious individuals & day\(^{-1}\) \\
\(\delta\)     & Disease-induced death rate of \(I\) and \(I_c\) & day\(^{-1}\) \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{table}
